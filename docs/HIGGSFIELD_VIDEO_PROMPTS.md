# PLAYBOOK — Higgsfield video prompts (Kling 3.0)

## Settings for every clip
- Model: Kling 3.0
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 720p
- Duration: as listed per clip
- Generations: 1 each; retry only if it fails the check below

## Negative prompt (paste into the negative field for every clip)
text, words, letters, numbers, jersey numbers, logos, brand names, watermark, signage, scoreboard graphics, subtitles, cartoon, illustration, CGI look, video game look, neon colours, extra limbs, extra fingers, distorted bodies, morphing faces, close-up faces, melting objects, duplicate balls, ball changing shape, flickering, camera shake, fisheye

## Check before keeping a clip
- People move naturally (no extra limbs, no warping), and the ball stays one ball.
- No text, numbers or logos anywhere.
- The play succeeds (the shot goes in, the point is won).
- The camera stays steady.

## Delivery
Save with the exact file names below and upload to the `videos` subfolder of the shared Drive folder.
