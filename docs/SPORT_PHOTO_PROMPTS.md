# Sport card photos: generation prompts

Ten photoreal images for the "Pick your sport" cards. Generate one image per sport
(ChatGPT, Midjourney, Higgsfield or similar), then upload them to the shared Google Drive folder.

## Delivery

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Square 1:1** (cards crop square on mobile and nearly square on desktop) |
| Size | At least **1024 × 1024** (2048 × 2048 if your tool allows) |
| Format | JPG, PNG or WebP |
| File names | `basketball.jpg`, `billiards.jpg`, `volleyball.jpg`, `badminton.jpg`, `pickleball.jpg`, `table-tennis.jpg`, `futsal.jpg`, `tennis.jpg`, `padel.jpg`, `sepak-takraw.jpg` |
| Where | A subfolder named **`sports`** in the Drive folder you shared before |

The site labels these cards "Illustrative", so AI images are never presented as real venue photos.
When a venue sends real photos, they replace these one at a time.

## How to prompt

For each sport, paste the **shared style block** first, then that sport's **scene line**.

### Shared style block (paste first, every time)

> Photorealistic sports photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field.
> Night, inside a premium modern sports venue: dark charcoal surroundings, warm overhead lighting pooling on the playing surface, faint haze in the air.
> The equipment is the hero. No people, no faces, no hands.
> Composition: the main subject sits in the **upper half of the frame, slightly right of centre**. The **lower 40% of the frame** is the playing surface fading into soft shadow, left clean as space for text.
> Unbranded equipment: no logos, no brand names, no text, no numbers, no watermarks.
> Colour: natural materials and deep charcoal shadows, with at most one small electric-lime (#D7FF3F) accent, such as a strip of court tape.
> Square 1:1 image.

## Quick quality check before uploading

- The subject is in the top half and the bottom of the image is darker and plain.
- No logos, numbers, text or watermarks anywhere (look closely at balls, rackets and nets).
- The equipment looks right: the pickleball has holes, the shuttlecock has feathers, the padel racket is solid with holes.
- There are no people or hands.
